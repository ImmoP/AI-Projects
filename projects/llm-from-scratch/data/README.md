# Educational text corpus

[`the-verdict.txt`](./the-verdict.txt) is the small educational text corpus used by the notebook for tokenization, dataloading, and language-model training experiments.

The underlying literary work is Edith Wharton's "The Verdict" (1908). Project Gutenberg includes the story in eBook No. 306, *The Early Short Fiction of Edith Wharton — Part 2*, and identifies its edition as public domain in the USA.

The immediate provenance of this repository's transcription is Sebastian Raschka's [`LLMs-from-scratch`](https://github.com/rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch) companion repository:

- pinned revision: `33f5b246766464910accf1c70e668811cfc4bf08`
- upstream path: [`ch02/01_main-chapter-code/the-verdict.txt`](https://github.com/rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch/blob/33f5b246766464910accf1c70e668811cfc4bf08/ch02/01_main-chapter-code/the-verdict.txt)

The local token sequence matches that pinned companion-repository file. It is not documented as a direct Project Gutenberg download or an exact Gutenberg transcription, and it may differ in formatting or transcription details from the current Gutenberg edition.
